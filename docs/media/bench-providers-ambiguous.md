# Provider benchmark

Generated by `node scripts/bench-providers.mjs` on 2026-09-20 02:37 UTC (Node v23.10.0, darwin). Every number below was measured in that run; nothing is estimated.

Task: a 10-field form of look-alike labels defined in `scripts/bench-providers.mjs` (an emergency contact's phone, a referrer's email, an employer's website, "First language", a manager's last name: all must map to `none`), 4 decisions per provider through the server's provider factories. Decision latency is wall clock around `provider.decide()`, so for Baseten it is the time until the K-th valid sample of the hedged vote, inside the production deadline of 2.3 s. Percentiles are nearest-rank, so with N=4 the p95 is the slowest successful call.
