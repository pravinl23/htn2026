# Provider benchmark

Generated by `node scripts/bench-providers.mjs` on 2026-09-20 02:50 UTC (Node v23.10.0, darwin). Every number below was measured in that run; nothing is estimated.

Task: the 12-field sample job application (`server/src/providers/sampleForm.ts`), 8 decisions per provider through the server's provider factories, and 4 streamed ghost-text drafts (at most 360 characters) per text provider through `POST /v1/ghost-text`. Decision latency is wall clock around `provider.decide()`, so for Baseten it is the time until the K-th valid sample of the hedged vote, inside the production deadline of 2.3 s. Percentiles are nearest-rank, so with N=8 the p95 is the slowest successful call.

## Notes

- **baseten**: K=3 samples + H=1 hedge per decision, thinking off. 2 of the successful decisions were partial votes (deadline or rate budget), which lowers their confidence. 11 straggler requests were aborted by the hedge.
- **typesafe**: Jev direct, calibrated.
- **llm (openai)**: one JSON-mode call, self-reported confidence.
- **heuristic**: no model: label keywords + autocomplete tokens; template drafts.
- Real calls in this run: 56 (guard: 120). Baseten calls were paced for a limit of 15 requests per minute.
- The heuristic answers in code, so its latency is the cost of the provider interface, not of a model.
